Research Topics

  • My research primarily lies in probabilistic modeling, learning and inference such as deep generative models. Recently, I concentrate on the intersection between probabilistic modeling and geometric learning, like the generative models for graphs and 3D structures.
  • With theoretical understanding, I’m also interested in doing research with a strong social impact and addressing fundamental problems of natural sciences, like computational chemistry/material/biology.
